What you get

Everything you need. Nothing you don't.

🛡️

Built-in Ad Blocker

Powered by the same engine as Brave Browser. Blocks ads, trackers, and malware domains with 137,000+ filter rules. No extensions needed.

🔒

HTTPS-Only Mode

Strict HTTPS enforcement by default. Insecure connections are blocked, not just warned about. Your data is always encrypted in transit.

👻

Anti-Fingerprinting

Canvas, WebGL, and AudioContext fingerprinting resistance built in. Websites can't build a unique profile of your browser to track you across the web.

🚫

Zero Telemetry

No usage analytics. No crash reports sent home. No "anonymous" data collection. Nothing leaves your machine. We literally can't see what you do.

⚡

Tiny & Fast

Under 10MB installed. Uses your OS's native webview instead of shipping an entire copy of Chromium. Starts instantly. Uses less memory.

🎨

Fully Customizable

Dark, Light, and Midnight (OLED) themes. Custom CSS injection. Configurable keybinds. A plain text config file you own. Your browser, your rules.

🔐

DNS over HTTPS

Built-in encrypted DNS with Quad9, Cloudflare, or Mullvad. Your ISP can't see your DNS queries or sell your browsing habits.

🌐

WebRTC Leak Protection

Prevents WebRTC from leaking your real IP address, even when using a VPN. Disabled for non-proxied connections by default.

Three levels of protection

Pick your comfort level. All of them are better than Chrome's defaults.

Standard

Everyday Browsing

  • Ad & tracker blocking (137K+ rules)
  • Referrer header stripping
  • Automatic HTTPS upgrades
  • Do Not Track & Global Privacy Control
  • Telemetry domain blocking
Strict
Recommended

Privacy-Focused

  • Everything in Standard, plus:
  • Third-party cookie blocking
  • WebRTC IP leak prevention
  • Canvas & WebGL fingerprint resistance
  • AudioContext spoofing
  • Tracking header stripping
Paranoid

Maximum Security

  • Everything in Strict, plus:
  • Full fingerprint resistance suite
  • JavaScript disabled by default
  • Timezone spoofing (reports UTC)
  • Font enumeration blocking
  • Screen dimension masking

Questions

Is Void really free?

Yes. Free as in freedom and free as in beer. GPL-3.0 licensed. No premium version. No paid features. Ever.

What engine does it use?

Void uses your operating system's native webview — WebView2 on Windows and WebKitGTK on Linux. No bundled Chromium.

Why is it so small?

Most browsers ship an entire copy of Chromium (~150MB). Void uses the webview your OS already has, so we only ship the browser chrome and privacy engine.

Will it break websites?

In Standard mode, almost never. Strict mode may block some third-party widgets. Paranoid mode disables JavaScript by default — some sites will need manual exceptions.

How do I report a bug?

Open an issue on our GitHub repository. We read every one.

Can I contribute?

Absolutely. Pull requests welcome. Check the README for build instructions and contribution guidelines.
