Every browser wants your data.
We want none of it.

Chrome sells your attention. Firefox adds AI. Safari locks you in. Brave has crypto. Arc has profiles of you.

Void has none of that. It's a browser. It browses. That's it. That's the product.

What you get

🛡️

Built-in Ad Blocker

Powered by the same engine as Brave. Blocks ads, trackers, and malware domains out of the box. No extensions needed.

🔒

HTTPS-Only Mode

Strict HTTPS enforcement by default. Insecure connections are blocked, not just warned about.

👻

Anti-Fingerprinting

Canvas, WebGL, and AudioContext fingerprinting resistance. Makes you look like everyone else.

🚫

Zero Telemetry

No usage analytics. No crash reports sent home. No "anonymous" data collection. Nothing leaves your machine.

⚡

Tiny & Fast

Under 10MB installed. Uses your OS's native webview instead of shipping an entire Chromium. Starts in milliseconds.

🎨

Customizable

Dark, Light, and Midnight (OLED) themes. Custom CSS injection. Configurable keybinds. Your browser, your rules.

🔐

DNS over HTTPS

Built-in DoH with Quad9, Cloudflare, or Mullvad. Your ISP can't see your DNS queries.

📋

Local Config

Everything stored in a plain TOML file on your machine. No account. No cloud. No sync. Just a file you own.

Three levels of paranoia

Pick your comfort level. All of them are better than Chrome's defaults.

Standard

Everyday Browsing

  • Ad & tracker blocking
  • Referrer stripping
  • HTTPS upgrades
  • DNT & GPC headers
Strict
Default

Privacy-Focused

  • Everything in Standard, plus:
  • Third-party cookie blocking
  • WebRTC leak prevention
  • Canvas fingerprint resistance
  • Telemetry domain blocking
Paranoid

Maximum Protection

  • Everything in Strict, plus:
  • Full fingerprint resistance
  • JavaScript disabled by default
  • Timezone spoofing
  • Font enumeration blocking
